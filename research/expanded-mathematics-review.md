# Expanded production review: mathematics, deep space and structures

Reviewed **all 180 designs** in the nine final production contact sheets, 20 variants per family. This review covers the enlarged `nocturne-compositions-v2` editions and supersedes the composition observations about the original centered editions in `mathematics-review.md`.

**Result: accepted without renderer changes.** Large motifs now occupy the frame, with deliberate edge crops, clear geometry and useful variation between variants. No blank output, unwanted raster rectangle, uniform gray background or unexpected sampling-domain cut was found. Repetition within each wallpaper is intentional. The 20 variants in each family visibly change geometry, rather than only changing color.

## Dense exceptions and 1080p behavior

The validator's 26 master black-fraction warnings correspond to these **13 designs in both native aspects**:

- Clifford Veils: 03, 04, 07, 08, 10, 11, 12, 14, 17 and 18.
- Torus Filaments: 09, 12 and 14.

Every one was inspected as a whole composition and in a 640 × 360 crop taken directly from its exported 1920 × 1080 PNG. These are intentional dense options. Clifford retains recognizable folded trajectories, bright contour concentrations and dark spaces; Torus retains interwoven ribbons and open black centers. Dense torus strands create a textile-like interference texture at 1080p, with finer separation in the larger exports. No muddy, flat gray background was observed. **Keep the warnings: no threshold or validation result was suppressed.**

Master black percentages do not describe every smaller export. Reducing a dense point or line pattern fills some very narrow black gaps. Direct measurement of the flagged 1080p files found **4.25% exact black for Clifford 11**, 4.59% for Clifford 14 and 5.99% for Clifford 04. Their mean RGB channel values are approximately 40.0, 44.8 and 39.1 out of 255 respectively. Full-frame inspection of Clifford 11 and 14 confirmed dark, finely textured compositions with clear luminous folds, rather than a uniform wash. They are substantially busier and have much less unlit area than the sparse families; they should not be described as having mostly black 1080p backgrounds or maximum OLED power savings. The collection can offer them as deliberate dense alternatives while retaining the measured exceptions.

## Inspection scope and scientific descriptions

All nine official `gallery/contact-sheets/<family>.jpg` sheets were reviewed. Additional direct full-frame inspections included Clifford Veils 11 and 14, Torus Filaments 12, Triangulated Spans 01 and Circuit Forests 18 at 1920 × 1080, and Remnant Shells 20 at 3440 × 1440. The 13 native detail crops are recorded in `previews/expanded-math-low-black-native-details.jpg`. This is a visual review of all 180 designs with selected native exports, not a claim that every one of their 900 resolution files was inspected individually.

The underlying models remain correctly distinguished from their artistic presentation. Clifford iteration, torus windings, damped harmonographs, thin point-mass lens mapping, Delaunay triangulation, geodesic subdivision and spanning-tree routing retain their mathematical basis. Galaxy and remnant-shell shapes are synthetic morphology; circuits are decorative. Repeated or scattered copies are a visual arrangement, not an interacting physical simulation. The expanded metadata states this distinction. Intentional display-edge cropping does not change those model claims or imply hydrodynamic, N-body, relativistic-ray-tracing or engineering validation.
