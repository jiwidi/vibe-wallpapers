# Production review: mathematics, deep space and structures

Reviewed all 20 production thumbnails in each of the nine contact sheets: Clifford Veils, Torus Filaments, Harmonograph Lace, Spiral Galaxies, Einstein Arcs, Remnant Shells, Triangulated Spans, Geodesic Pavilions and Circuit Forests. This covers all 180 designs.

**Result: no generator changes or wallpaper re-renders required.** Principal forms remain complete, with black margins and meaningful geometric variation. Every family has 20 distinct parameter sets and 20 distinct 5120-pixel master hashes; all 180 master hashes differ. Recorded model parameters agree between standard and ultrawide versions.

Native-output inspection included:

- 1920 × 1080: Clifford Veils 02, Circuit Forests 03 and Einstein Arcs 12.
- 3440 × 1440: Clifford Veils 02, Remnant Shells 20 and Triangulated Spans 19.

The quiet Clifford and circuit designs are intentionally sparse and appear darker in the small contact sheets. Their native 1080p linework, pads and trajectories remain legible. The tested ultrawide images retain complete central forms and substantial peripheral black space. The lattice boundaries are intentional model edges, not accidental image clipping.

An additional numerical audit reconstructed all 20 Einstein Arc fields using their production seeds. The highest brightness on any sampling boundary was **0.0007349189**, versus the lowest rendered contour **0.045** (1.633% of that contour threshold, worst case variant 12). Thus no displayed lens isophote reaches the computational boundary. Remnant shells are projected parametric geometry, and their finite polar sampling does not produce a visible cut edge.

Scientific wording was checked against implementation. Clifford maps, torus winding numbers, damped harmonographs, point-mass lens equations, Delaunay triangulation, geodesic subdivision and spanning-tree construction are identified accurately. Galaxies and remnant shells are explicitly synthetic morphology; circuits are decorative; no hydrodynamic, N-body, relativistic-ray-tracing or structural-engineering validation is implied.

The final 1600 × 1020 **Geodesic Pavilions** contact sheet was re-opened after the gallery rebuild. All labels and variant numbers 01–20 are complete and legible, with no overlap. The earlier typography observation is resolved; no outstanding issues remain from this review.
